# Delhi NCR Commercial Office Fit-Out & Building NOC Checklist

**Status: draft structure only - not approved for distribution.**

## Intended PDF structure

1. Purpose, scope, and disclaimer
2. Project brief and building-information record
3. Building-management coordination checklist
4. Drawings, fit-out scope, and consultant-coordination record
5. Site-access, working-hours, and material-handling record
6. Health, safety, insurance, and contractor-document record
7. Fire, MEP, and statutory-review record
8. Pre-handover and close-out record
9. Contact and escalation record

## Approval requirements before publication

- Legal/compliance review of every checklist item and disclaimer.
- Building-management verification: requirements differ by property and authority.
- Business approval of brand, contact details, lead follow-up process, and document version.
- Final designed PDF supplied or approved for production.

No statement in this draft is a regulatory requirement or legal advice.
